# Content Decisions

The original checklist below is resolved by the approved `MUNITIONS.md`
handoff. Runtime assumptions discovered by Phase 1 are tracked separately in
`PHASE1_FINDINGS.md` and must be resolved before weapon definitions are cloned.

## Initial Release

- Number of munitions in v0.1.0
- Included categories and specific battlefield roles
- Fictional naming convention and manufacturer/faction identity
- Whether variants share a family name or use independent designations

## Behavior and Balance

- Vanilla reference weapon for each new munition
- Intended target types and guidance method
- Effective range, propulsion, maneuverability, and seeker limitations
- Warhead yield, armor penetration, fuzing, and area effects
- Mass, drag, radar signature, price, and rack capacity
- Intended strengths, drawbacks, and overlap with vanilla or installed mods

## Availability

- Compatible vanilla and modded aircraft
- Allowed hardpoints and rack sizes per aircraft
- Whether multiplayer servers and clients must install the same package
- Whether AI factions receive access by default

## Presentation

- Vanilla mesh chosen for each v0.1.0 remix
- Scale, material, tint, markings, icon, launch audio, and effects
- Encyclopedia descriptions and player-facing stat text

## Acceptance Criteria

- Desired realism level: vanilla-balanced, realistic, or intentionally arcade
- Test missions and measurable success thresholds for each weapon role
- Compatibility policy for other munitions packs
