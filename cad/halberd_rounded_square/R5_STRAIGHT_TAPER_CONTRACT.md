# R5 — straight housing edges, uniform rear taper, black occlusion backs

Date: 2026-09-23. User correction: annotations are intent guides; their irregularities must not become geometry. Intended long edges are straight and the full rear taper has a constant rate. Only the back faces of intake/nozzle recesses should become darker; their sides retain the R4 treatment.

## Grounding

- The visible wave is generated by R3's nine-section `ruled=False` loft, inherited by R4. It is not a modeled panel seam. R5 removes that interpolation instead of smoothing it again.
- Kris reference inspected directly: `cad/kris/IRM-S4_Kris_PL10_Hybrid.step`, `cad/kris/Kris_hybrid_aft_closeup.png`, `cad/kris/Kris_hybrid_end.png`, and `cad/kris/generate_kris_hybrid.py` / `generate_pl10_stencil.py`. The inherited `aft_dark_recess` reads essentially black in the reference images. Its source shade is `#33383A`; this is appearance evidence, not a reason to copy a numerically lighter shade onto Halberd.
- Use explicit pure-black rear-cap geometry as an occlusion proxy. Preserve the lighter/depth-readable nozzle funnels and all intake side-wall colors. Confirm both rendered and solid CAD views.

## Geometry decisions

- Retain the inlet footprint, curved floor, canted entry and 45-degree clocking.
- Use only three controlling outer sections: stage seam, the existing passage transition at X=210 mm, and the forward inlet construction end at X=715 mm. Connect them with ruled geometry, eliminating spline overshoot.
- From X=210 to the seam, shoulder width, roof width and roof height each vary linearly between their endpoint values: one constant-rate rear taper. The forward inlet region has straight sides/roof edges.
- Keep the narrow terminal section at the seam, the accepted nose/booster, the mounted fin's actual shape and pose, and the previously delivered channel clear back at X=-444.973923 mm.
- Keep the curved interface where the housing actually meets the rounded body. Straight design edges do not imply flattening the airframe itself.
- Separate the intake rear cap from its lining without changing their combined geometry. Recolor that cap and both nozzle back caps only. Black material settings deliberately emulate obscuration, not a physical metal specification.

## Verification targets

1. Exported rear roof widths/heights match endpoint interpolation at many stations; long upper edges have no curvature/oscillation.
2. Inlet remains open with intact floor/roof/flanks; no new holes from narrowing the housing.
3. Fin remains seated on the housing, main/booster stage ownership unchanged, and accepted non-housing components match R4 exactly.
4. Three rear caps serialize as opaque black; side/funnel materials match R4. Intake cap plus lining equals the prior cup geometry.
5. Strict validation and direct review of plan/taper, close edge, intake-back, main/booster nozzle and separated views. No inherited numerical score or automatic fourfold propagation.

### Verification

`src/check_intake_r5.py` passes against exported geometry:

- Ten rear cross-sections match the same linear roof-height/roof-width equations within 0.002 mm. Four forward samples match the straight inlet segment. Both rear upper edges and both forward upper edges have measured chord/curve deviation below 0.002 mm (numerical results are effectively zero).
- The duct remains enclosed at seven axial sections after reshaping; the original core is retained. The mounted fin still has 1523.67 mm3 of housing contact, and its geometry/pose are unchanged.
- The intake lining plus its newly separated rear cap exactly equals the R4 cup geometry. Its clear back plane remains X=-444.973923 mm; the existing 544.973923 mm extension is preserved.
- Intake lining and nozzle-funnel colors exactly match R4 readback. Only three rear caps serialize as opaque RGBA `(0,0,0,1)`: intake back, main nozzle back, booster nozzle back. The intake cap is a separate solid so its side lining is not recolored.
- Kris `aft_dark_recess` was read from the explicit hybrid STEP and compared with its saved aft views; Halberd uses pure black to produce that occluded appearance in both CAD display modes, not a claim that its old numeric shade was copied.
- Both native strict every-placement validations pass: 17 occurrences / 12 prototypes each, zero failures. All separated shapes match after reversing the review offset.

Evidence: `reviews/intake_R5_checks.json`, two fact reports and `reviews/intake_R5_manifest.json`. All eleven final snapshots were directly inspected through the three boards above. Solid-mode edge views confirm the wave is gone; solid/rendered back-face views show black backs with gray sides.

The straight design boundaries are distinct from the genuinely curved junction with the rounded airframe. No attempt is made to flatten the core or curved inlet floor. The rear segment is now a single constant-rate taper, not a smoothed approximation through the earlier sketch-derived stations.
