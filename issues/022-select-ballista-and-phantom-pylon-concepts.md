## Slice

Deliver two user-selection packets, one for Ballista's external single carriage
and one for Phantom's external single carriage, using issue 020's measured rack
and station evidence. Each packet presents three architectural directions around
the fixed stowed weapon envelope and records a selected direction or hybrid.
Internal and multi-round Ballista variants remain visibly separate deferred work.

## Ownership and evidence

- Preserve Ballista's approved missile CAD and Phantom's active visual-reboot
  sources. Do not silently treat the historical R5 Phantom Unity candidate as the
  current munition master.
- Use measured external donor contexts only. Keep internal-bay, twin and triple
  context out of the external-single concept silhouettes except for a clearly
  labeled incompatibility/deferment panel.
- Primary direct visual review and user concept selection are required.

## Acceptance

1. Ballista concepts preserve the fixed 2594.424 mm length, 322.805 mm stowed
   envelope, authored forward/aft shoe datums and folded-wing mechanism clearance.
2. Phantom concepts preserve the fixed 2800 mm length and 250 mm stowed envelope,
   use the current selected body/wing/tail/intake baseline, and reserve the known
   wing-cover, pin-exit, tail-pocket, intake and RF regions.
3. Each weapon compares a tapered rail, exposed ejector/mechanism beam, and
   semi-conformal cradle/recessed adapter as distinct architectures.
4. Ballista's packet makes the heavy dual-lug/ejector load path readable and
   explicitly marks x2, triple and internal carriage as different future racks.
5. Phantom's packet makes release-before-deployment legible and does not add a
   pylon contact over a moving cover or invent attachment geometry where issue
   020 found none.
6. Packets contain all-side, mounted-context, clearance, release-direction and
   representative-distance views with critical dimensions and evidence labels.
7. User selections and exact approval boundaries are recorded without advancing
   either weapon beyond `concept-approved` for its pylon.

## Test notes

- Verify saved concept solids, fixed envelope identity and claimed interface
  contacts/clearances.
- Review both stowed and immediate-release context; deployed appendages alone do
  not establish carriage fit.
- A missing Phantom whole-airframe decision remains a blocker for production and
  is not bypassed by approving a pylon direction.
